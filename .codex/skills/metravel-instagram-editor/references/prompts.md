# Instagram task prompts

Copy the matching prompt and supply only inputs that are known. Resolve an
open-browser reference from actual CUA state. These prompts request preparation
or read-only review; append a concrete save/publish instruction only when that
action is intended. They do not carry approvals between sessions.

## Audit a post

> Use $metravel-instagram-editor to inspect the Instagram @metravelby post open
> in my browser for [date in Europe/Warsaw]. Read all its slides and caption;
> identify the source article or project quest and verify it. For every slide,
> compare the picture, text and actual location/quest step. Report the mismatch
> with source evidence and prepare a concrete visual brief. Keep account state,
> caption and schedule unchanged during this audit.

## Remake images of a quest post

> Use $metravel-instagram-editor and $metravel-instagram-visuals to remake images
> for [scheduled-post identity/date or local carousel path]. Read the current
> post and the full source quest, then create a slide-to-step source manifest.
> Show the real city and quest activity, with confirmed landmark references and
> the legend as a restrained illustrated motif. Use built-in imagegen, retain
> approved wording and layout dimensions, inspect each generated scene and
> final slide, and deliver all final files plus a contact sheet and generation
> prompts. Identify illustrations explicitly. If I have authorized saving this
> scheduled post, verify that the UI supports media replacement and preserve its
> caption and time; otherwise deliver the ready replacement pack. Do not delete
> or recreate the scheduled entry as a workaround.

## Create a carousel

> Use $metravel-instagram-editor to prepare a carousel about [topic/source URL].
> Use only published, moderated author-1 articles or confirmed project quests.
> Choose a coherent slide sequence, verify each number and each photo's place,
> use the existing carousel builder and brand footer, and produce slides,
> caption, one CTA, alt-text draft, geotag, source manifest and preview. If
> illustrations are requested, use $metravel-instagram-visuals. Prepare a draft
> without posting or changing the schedule.

## Prepare a weekly content pack

> Use $metravel-instagram-editor to prepare the @metravelby pack for [absolute
> start–end dates, Europe/Warsaw]. Read the current calendar and strategy first;
> preserve its latest owner decisions. Verify sources and media for each unit,
> prepare carousels, Reel scripts/live-footage lists, captions, story ideas and
> a proposed schedule. Record source-backed facts, ready files and precise
> missing material; do not invent footage or a visit. Update only the relevant
> plan rows if calendar maintenance is included. Present previews for review
> without publishing or creating an automation.

## Review the pack and results

> Use $metravel-instagram-editor to review [post/pack path] and account metrics
> for [absolute start–end dates]. Review source claims, photograph identity,
> illustration disclosure, slide readability, spoilers, brand/footer, caption
> and schedule. Obtain only available measured statistics through the existing
> inspected insights script or visible CUA UI; distinguish missing data and
> hypotheses. Return per-item pass/fail, raw evidence, precise repairs and
> readiness for publication. Keep account state unchanged.
