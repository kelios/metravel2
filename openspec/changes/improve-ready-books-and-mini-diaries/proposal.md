## Why

The mobile catalogue hides its main action below the first screen. Download labels do not match the separate book and cover files. Search signals justify a limited trial of two short A5 diaries and a check of rights for the precise public editions.

## What Changes

- Bring the catalogue choice forward, group country styles, and keep the books entry reachable in the desktop header.
- Name downloads by purpose and component. Improve related entry points and measure genuine requests.
- Inventory rights for existing revisions and require evidence before publishing new ones.
- Prepare two independent six-page A5 diaries with separately verified PDF and preview resources.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `ready-books-discovery-and-mini-diaries`: Catalogue choice, exact download labels, related discovery, rights checks, and a compact pilot format.

### Modified Capabilities

None in the main specs. The earlier full-book contract remains valid.

## Impact

Platform impact: mobile web and desktop web. Localization impact: RU/BE/UK/PL/EN UI; Russian editorial PDFs.
Accessibility: semantic links, labelled choices, keyboard access. SEO: retain existing slugs, canonicals and SSR, verify search availability.
Data: additive pilot publication metadata; no backend API change. Security: revision-bound rights and download integrity. Performance: existing thin catalogue and lazy covers. Analytics: consent-aware typed events, no personal input.
Non-goals: new country volumes, payments, a print service, native exposure and claims of professional print readiness. Source gaps are reported; use no fabricated material. Production storage upload follows the owner-terminal gate.
