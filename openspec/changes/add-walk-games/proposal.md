## Why

Visitors need a ready-to-play activity for a walk together. The two existing MeTravel decks can serve both people using one phone and people taking printed cards.

## What Changes

- Add a public Games and quizzes section with a kids' deck and an adults' deck.
- Offer a shuffled, single-device walk and an A4 print deck for each game.
- Offer printable coloring sheets based on author photographs in the corresponding articles and a combined PDF in the catalog.
- Add travel quizzes for Belarus, Poland and the world, and optional tasks tied to article route stops.
- Publish playable content, print files and previews in S3.
- Platform impact: mobile and desktop web. Localization impact: RU/BE/UK/PL/EN interface, Russian editorial cards and PDFs.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `walk-games`: discover and play a walk deck or download it to print.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Public navigation, localized metadata and keyboard-accessible controls. Progress is local to the page. Published game data is fetched from S3-backed versioned resources. No account, GPS requirement or multiplayer synchronization is needed.
